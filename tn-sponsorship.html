Looking for Qualified TN Visa Candidates

Astir Services LLC has extensive experience providing TN visa sponsorship.

Astir Services is constantly looking for highly qualified individuals who wish to pursue a lucrative career in information technology. The TN visa sponsorship program has been an integral part of our recruitment strategy for many years. If you are a Mexican or Canadian national who is looking for a TN sponsor company, and have the minimum of a Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science or a related discipline, please contact us today.

The TN visa is a special classification for citizens of Canada and Mexico as part of the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA). USMCA replaced the North American Free Trade Agreement (NAFTA) on July 1, 2020.

If you are looking for a TN sponsor, and are interested in lucrative employment as an IT consultant in the U.S., please contact Astir Services LLC today. We offer competitive salaries, an extensive suite of benefits, and many relationships with top prime vendors and direct clients.
