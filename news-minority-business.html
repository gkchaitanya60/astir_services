Astir IT Solutions Inc. Has been Recognized as One of New Jersey’s Top Minority-Owned Businesses by NJBIZ Astir IT Solutions, Inc., a leader in IT Consulting, Outsourcing, and Software Development, has been selected by the NJBIZ as one of New Jersey’s Top Minority-Owned Businesses.

July 6, 2026 – South Plainfield, N.J. – Astir IT Solutions, Inc. is pleased to announce that it has been recognized among the Top Minority-Owned Businesses New Jersey by NJBiz. This recognition is provided by one of New Jersey’s top business journals, and is based on an evaluation of company revenue, growth, and employee count.

“We are extremely proud that our hard work and dedication has been recognized.” Said Robert Markowitz Astir IT Solutions, Inc. Executive Vice President. “Our philosophy continues to be, to invest in our employees by hiring and training the very best IT professionals, which enables us to provide our clients with the highest quality of services possible. This in turn, positions us for even greater success in the future.”

About Astir IT Solutions, Inc.

Founded in 2001, The Astir Family of Companies [includes Astir IT Solutions, Inc.(www.astirit.com), Astir Services (www.astirservices.net), Astir Analytics (www.astiranalytics.com), Astir Technologies (www.astir.tech), Active Alliance Systems Corporation (www.activealliancecorp.com), and Astir Ventures (www.astir.vc)] provides clients with award winning IT consulting, outsourcing, and application development services. Our products and solutions are designed to solve complex business problems, increase operational efficiency, and enable clients to leverage technology for great business gains. The flagship, Astir IT Solutions, Inc., began as a home business with 2 employees. With each passing year, Astir has grown exponentially, and today is a multinational corporation, servicing clients across the U.S. and the world.
