Business experience and technical expertise, brought together around your priorities. We work closely with you—from understanding your needs to building solutions and preparing your people to use them.

SecurityProtection built into delivery.

Our development center, data warehouses, and hosts all employ the most advanced forms of data and physical security possible. Our development center is protected by a complete access control system, including restricted mobile device usage, power backups, and temperature controls. Additionally, our warehousing and hosting systems are secured using multiple layers of digital security.

We utilize cutting edge security methodologies, procedures, and tools throughout every step of the development and data management processes. Our personnel are experts in the latest IT security technologies and will architect according to your exact specifications. We ensure that the IT services and solutions we provide customers are highly secure and reliable.

Dedicated ResourcesThe right people, committed to you.

To make sure we have the best and brightest IT talent available on the market, we offer generous compensation packages and employ a rigorous screening process. When we have a customer engagement, we hand-pick a team of technical experts that are perfect matches for the exact business requirement. That team is then dedicated to that customer as an extension of their in-house IT organization. We are proud to have an extraordinarily high staff retention rate, which adds to our long-term stability and reputation for both employee and customer satisfaction. We are also proud that our workforce is 100% employee based.

Cost-EfficiencyFlexible delivery. Thoughtful investment.

Our streamlined processes, efficient business model, and flexible IT solutions enable us to provide extremely cost-effective pricing. When becoming an Astir Services customer, we offer you the IT resources and expertise you need, without taking on the associated costs of hiring full-time employees. For those with tighter budget restrictions, our offshore solutions provide further savings.

QualityCare in every stage of the work.

For Astir Services, quality is paramount. We have built a reputation on delivering top quality IT products and services to our customers. Working closely with customers and fully understanding their needs, coupled with our extensive expertise and experience, enables us to provide the highest quality IT solutions. We utilize a flexible business model, with onsite, offsite, and offshore options for all our IT products, services, and solutions.

E-Verify CertificationResponsible employment practices.

Astir Services LLC is certified by E-Verify, the U.S. Department of Homeland Security’s electronic employment eligibility verification system. This is a top-rated application tracking system that integrates with a number of federal programs. This voluntary program allows employers to electronically verify employee information against federal databases. Having an E-Verify certification allows us to further streamline the hiring process, maintain a legal workforce, improves the accuracy of wage and tax reporting, and eliminates Social Security mismatches. This translates into a more efficient human resources system, freeing up resources so we can maintain a more cost-effective pricing model.
