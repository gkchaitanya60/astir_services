Fully Automate Your Back and Front Office

As your business grows, your everyday workflows and processes will become increasingly cumbersome on your team. In order to free up resources, streamline processes, and increase accuracy, you need automation tools. Finding the right set of tools may be difficult, as there are numerous options available… including Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) solutions. There are ERP tools that automate individual processes, the processes across an entire department, or all processes across multiple business areas. With this wide array of choices available to you, it can be exceedingly difficult to understand exactly what ERP tool or suite best suits your unique business needs.

Astir Services LLC can help you better understand your front and back office automation needs. Working closely with customers, we analyze business requirements and help recommend the best ERP solution(s) needed to meet all business needs. Astir has the expertise and experience necessary to implement, integrate, and manage any ERP solution, so your business can refocus resources where they matter the most: growing your business.

A CLOSER LOOK

Expertise across ERP platforms.

Microsoft Dynamics 365: Microsoft is a major player in the ERP market, and has been for many years. Starting with a suite of ERP tools, including Dynamics, AX, NAV, SL, etc., the Dynamics 365 platform is the next evolutionary step. As a modular cloud based application, Microsoft Dynamics 365 offers customers both back and front office automation. With modules ranging from field operations, marketing, sales, project management, etc., you can automate your entire business.

Microsoft Dynamics 365 integrates with other Microsoft products, including Office, Outlook, PowerBI, etc., offering expanded feature functionality and seamless implementation into your existing technology environment. Microsoft Dynamics 365 also includes multiple mobile applications, so you and your employees can conduct business from anywhere, at any time.

Salesforce: Salesforce is the golden standard for sales and marketing automation. Holding the largest market share and boasting a large network of 3rd party feature functionality expansions, Salesforce can offer a powerful customer relationship management (CRM) solution to businesses of all sizes.

Pardot: Pardot, offered by Salesforce, is an enterprise level sales and marketing automation tool. With a large partner network and award-winning service, large businesses can easily manage their sales and marketing workflows.

Pega Systems: Pega Systems has entered the front and back office automation market with an enterprise level customer relationship management (CRM) solution.
