Introduction

At Astir Services LLC our primary concern is maintaining our relationship with our customers. We are committed to securing our customers’ data and ensure its’ confidentiality. This Privacy Policy will explain exactly what type of information is collected from our customers and how it is used. Henceforth, “we,” “us” and “our” will refer to Astir Services, and “you” and “your” shall refer to the customer.

Information We Collect

At any time, you may visit our website anonymously and learn about the services Astir Services offers.

We collect two general types of information, namely personal information and aggregate data. As used in this Policy, the term “personal information” means information that specifically identifies an individual (such as a name and email address), and demographic and other information when directly linked to information that can identify an individual. Our definition of personal information does not include “aggregate” data.

Aggregate data is information we collect about a group or category of services or users from which user identities have been removed. In other words, no personal information is included in aggregate data. Aggregate data helps us understand trends in our users’ needs so that we can better consider new features or otherwise tailor our services. This policy in no way restricts or limits our collection and use of aggregate data, and we may share aggregate data about our users with third parties for various purposes, including to help us better understand our customer needs and improve our services and for advertising and marketing purposes.

When you visit our website, our servers automatically record certain information that your browser sends whenever you visit a website. This information may include, but is not limited to, your computer’s internet protocol (IP) address, browser type, the website you viewed prior to viewing Astir Services, and the information you searched for on our site.

Cookies, Clear GIF’s, Tracking Software, and Analysis We may use “cookies” and other tracking software to store session information. This will be determined by the type of services required, and will include the storage of a small amount of data on your computer hard drive as an identifier. Astir Services may set and require access to said cookies remotely in order to render certain services. You can instruct your web browser, by changing its’ options, to stop accepting cookies or to prompt you before accepting a cookie from any website. If you choose to limit the cookies imprinted onto your computer, certain aspects of the Astir Services website may not function properly.

Disclosure of Information

All collected data will remain private and confidential.

At no time will any collected information be bought, sold, rented, exchanged, or otherwise shared by any 3rd party entity. Astir Services will, however, share any collected information in order to respond to and comply with investigations, court orders or any legal process required by law. This will include investigation and prevention of illegal activities, suspected fraud, and potential threats to personal safety, or violations of the Astir Services Terms and Conditions of Use. In the event of a sale or merger resulting in a 3rd party acquiring Astir Services; all the personal and aggregate data stored in Astir Services will be transferred to the new ownership. The customer retains all rights and intellectual property to all information entered into Astir Services.

Security

Astir Services takes reasonable steps to protect the information we collect from you to prevent loss, misuse, and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

Emails and Notifications

If you wish to subscribe to our monthly newsletter, we will use your name and email address to send the newsletter to you. Our newsletters contain important information such as updates and enhancements to our services. Out of respect for your privacy, we provide you a way to unsubscribe.

3rd Party Links and Blogs

At any point, on the Astir Services website, its’ direct affiliates, and/or any other Astir Services marketing materials, Astir Services retains the right to provide links to like-minded, 3rd party entities. The customer should be aware that once they navigate to that 3rd party link, Astir Services is no longer responsible for the content, privacy and security of that link. Any customer who posts on blogs or any other forums associated with Astir Services should be aware that 3rd parties could use any information contained therein. Astir Services is not responsible for any information posted on these forums, as 3rd parties may collect and utilize it for unsolicited emails.

Updating Personal Information

At any time, the customer is free to update, change or modify your personal information through our user profile module. If for any reason you have difficulty changing this information, please contact us at info@astirservices.net

Policy Changes

Astir Services retains the right to change this Privacy Policy at any time and will place notification of any change on the Astir Services website. There is also a date that appears on the top of this page, which will reflect the most recent date of change to the policy.

Questions

If you have any questions, comments, concerns or feedback in reference to this Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, or any other privacy or security concern, please contact us at info@astirservices.net .
