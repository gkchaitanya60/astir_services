DevOps methodologies and tools have become some of the hottest technologies on the market. Explore the details

DevOps is the next evolutionary step for Agile Systems Administration/Agile Operations. DevOps continues to break down the walls that traditionally exist between different segments of an IT project by implementing common tools and fostering communication. DevOps can help bridge the gaps that normally exist between stakeholders, business analysts, developers, and software quality assurance testers. By utilizing DevOps methodologies and adopting DevOps technologies, your IT projects will be more flexible, scalable, and will be delivered faster and more reliably.

Astir Services has extensive experience with DevOps, and have used DevOps methodologies and technologies to successfully deliver and manage customer IT projects. We work close with customers, keeping them involved throughout the various phases of a development project so we can continually capture and implement customer input. This nimble, flexible process ensures that the development project constantly evolves with the needs of the customer, so the end product us a perfect match for customer needs. Our IT consultants are experts in the latest, most cutting edge DevOps technologies, and offer customers rapid, incremental delivery of highly scalable applications.
