How we can help.

Astir Services has an IT training and project placement program designed specifically for the unique needs of F1 visa holders.

For highly qualified individuals who are on F1 OPT, CPT, or STEM Extension status, Astir Services offers a specialized IT training and project placement program. This program is designed to support students through their OPT STEM extension, and provide them with the IT and soft skills training they need to build a solid foundation for a long, successful IT consulting career. Astir also provides potential H1B sponsorship to qualified F1 candidates.

Astir Services is always looking for qualified individuals to join our award winning team. We need to train and hire the best and brightest to keep up with the high volume of unique projects we have with customers.
