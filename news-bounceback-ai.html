BounceBack AI Launches to Turn Email Bounces into Revenue Opportunities

New AI platform identifies the people behind hard bounces, tracks career moves, and helps companies reconnect with buyers when they are most open to new solutions

NEW YORK, NY – September 8, 2026 – BounceBack AI (www.bouncebackai.ai) today announced the launch of its AI-powered platform that transforms hard email bounces into actionable sales and marketing opportunities.

  • Identify the individual behind a hard-bounced email
  • Track job changes, promotions, and company moves
  • Detect when contacts enter decision-making roles
  • Re-engage prospects at key career transition points
  • Recover pipeline opportunities that would otherwise be lost
Explore the details

Created through a joint venture between Nurture Marketing and Astir IT Solutions, Inc., BounceBack AI, which is patent pending helps organizations identify individuals behind bounced email addresses, track their career transitions, and reconnect with them when they move into new roles, often with greater influence and purchasing authority.

"Most companies view hard bounces as lost contacts," said Eric Rabinowitz, Co-Founder of BounceBack AI and CEO of Nurture Marketing. "We see them as signals of change. When people take new positions, they're often evaluating new vendors, technologies, and partners. BounceBack AI helps companies engage at exactly the right moment."

Rather than allowing valuable relationships to disappear through database decay, BounceBack AI uses artificial intelligence to uncover where contacts have gone, identify promotions and job changes, and alert revenue teams when former contacts become potential buyers in new organizations.

"The result is a practical solution that helps organizations uncover revenue opportunities that most companies never realize exist."

Designed for marketing, sales, and RevOps teams, BounceBack AI integrates with existing workflows and enables faster action on emerging opportunities.
