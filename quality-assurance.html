THE OPPORTUNITY

Making Sure Your Technology Operates Perfectly

Astir Services LLC has a series of comprehensive quality assurance (QA) and testing services that can test software applications, products, commercial solutions, devices, networks, infrastructures, protocols, etc.

Our businesses rely heavily on technology, which must work correctly all the time, or your bottom line will suffer. Our expert quality assurance (QA) resources can help you understand your testing needs, and will custom design the solutions and services that will help keep your business running at peak efficiency. Astir Testing as a Service (TaaS) solutions cover applications, software, mobile devices, mobile apps, networks, operating systems, and much more.

Astir Services goes well beyond the traditional quality assurance testing offered by our competitors. Regardless of what platforms, frameworks, operating systems, or devices used by your business, Astir will support you throughout every stage. Our custom designed quality assurance testing services will identify problems, validate processes and procedures, highlight areas for improvement, and ensure that your technology environment remains operational… which will keep you in business.

CAPABILITIES & EXPERTISE

Testing, from every angle.

  • Test Consultation, Strategy, and Planning
  • Benchmarking
  • Automation Testing
  • Lifecycle Testing
  • System Performance Testing and Validation
  • Operating System Testing and Porting Validation
  • WI-FI and Network Testing
  • Device Testing
  • Localization Testing
  • Load Testing
  • And Much More

Our testing services are designed to stress your technology environment in two specific scenarios: real-world scenarios that mimic your everyday workloads and high-volume testing that stresses systems beyond your current parameters. This will ensure that not only will your applications, products, networks, etc. handle the workloads you currently have, but are scalable to accommodate your future growth.

Astir Services specializes in both automation and manual quality assurance (QA) testing. If your business needs to test the user experience of your latest software product, or needs to test the back-end for scalability, stability, and security, Astir has the services and solutions you need.
