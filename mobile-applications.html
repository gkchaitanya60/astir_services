THE OPPORTUNITY

The Mobility Solutions Your Business Needs

Mobile devices have revolutionized the way we conduct business. Using smartphones and tablet PCs have enabled us to conduct business from anywhere, at any time. This unprecedented flexibility can be leveraged to grow your business exponentially, as with the right tools and level of access, you will never miss another opportunity. To properly take advantage of available mobile devices, your infrastructure, networks, systems, applications, etc. must be mobile friendly.

CAPABILITIES & EXPERTISE

How we can help.

Having the right mobile solutions in place can mean the difference between success and failure in today’s business world.

Astir Services LLC has the mobile development and testing services necessary to help your business become more agile. We have extensive experience developing complex business intelligence applications for mobile devices, including both Android and iOS operating systems. Astir can develop your new mobile app from scratch, or create and optimize a mobile version of your existing web applications. Our mobile services and solutions will enable you and your employees to conduct business from any mobile device, at any time, at any location… We can help take your business mobile.

Astir Services can custom design, develop, test, integrate, implement, and manage mobile apps for any mobile operating system; including Windows, iOS, and Android. We have extensive experience developing on both smartphones and tablet PCs.

Examples of the mobile apps we have developed for customers include:

  • Marketing Campaign Management
  • Email Campaign Management
  • Social Media Measurement
  • Corporate News/Information Sharing
  • Sales Funnel Measurement
  • Medication Alerts
  • Healthcare Data Analysis
